# AdBrain off-page / GEO playbook — the part that can't be coded

**Why this document exists.** Across a 75,000-brand study, *brand mentions on other websites* correlated
about **3x more strongly with AI-answer visibility than backlinks**, and stronger than anything on your own
pages. On-page SEO (which is now done well) only decides *which of your qualifying pages* gets cited — being
mentioned and consistently described *around the web* is what makes you qualify at all. None of this is code.
It is founder-led work, and it is the single highest-leverage thing left. This is the sequence.

The rule underneath all of it: **be genuinely present and consistently described on the sources that AI
engines trust.** No spam, no fake reviews, no bought links — those get you excluded, not cited.

---

## Tier 1 — Do this month (high value, low effort, no writing skill needed)

### 1. Claim your review-site and directory listings
Brands present on 4+ of these are reportedly ~2.8x more likely to be named in ChatGPT answers, and review
pages are heavily cited for commercial queries.
- **G2, Capterra, GetApp, TrustRadius** — create the company + product profile. Category: "Advertising / Marketing Analytics" or "PPC / Paid Ads Software."
- **Product Hunt** — a launch (even a modest one) creates a durable, high-authority mention page.
- **Crunchbase** — company profile with founders, funding, description, website.
- **Trustpilot** — claim the profile (don't solicit fake reviews; just claim it so the entity exists).
- Use the **exact same** company name, one-line description, and website URL on every one (see Tier 3).

### 2. Create a Wikidata entry (~30 minutes)
Google ingests Wikidata directly into its Knowledge Graph. At wikidata.org, create an item: instance-of
"software company / SaaS", industry, founder(s), HQ, official website, and any identifiers (Crunchbase, G2).
This is realistic for a startup today. (Wikipedia itself needs notability — don't force that yet.)

### 3. Turn on unlinked-mention monitoring
Set a **Google Alert** (and, if you have it, an Ahrefs/Semrush alert) for "AdBrain" + "AdBrain AI." Every time
someone mentions you without linking, email them a two-line thank-you asking for a link. ~10-15% say yes, and
the *mention itself already helps* AI regardless of the link.

---

## Tier 2 — The founder engine (this is where the real mentions come from)

### 4. Founder-led expert quotes (HARO-style)
Sign up for a journalist-request service (Connectively/HARO, Qwoted, Featured). Answer 2-3 relevant queries a
week about Meta/Google ads, creative testing, ad measurement. Each accepted answer = a mention (often a link)
on a real publication. This is the fastest path to third-party editorial mentions for a new brand.

### 5. Podcasts and guest content
Pitch 1-2 DTC / performance-marketing / SaaS podcasts a month as a guest. Each episode page is a durable,
transcribed mention. Same for a guest article on a marketing blog. You already have the raw material — the 11
blog articles are your talking points.

### 6. Get onto "best-of" and comparison lists
Comparison and listicle content accounts for a large share of AI citations ("best X for Y" pages are among the
most-cited). Find "best Meta ads tools / best ad reporting software 2026" articles and email the authors to be
considered/added. Also publish your own honest comparison content on the blog.

### 7. Genuinely helpful answers on Reddit / Quora (authentic only)
Reddit appears in roughly 1 in 5 AI answers. In subreddits like r/PPC, r/FacebookAds, r/agency, answer real
questions with genuine value and mention AdBrain only where it honestly fits. **Coordinated or promotional
posting backfires and risks future exclusion** — this is slow-burn, human, and easy to do badly. One good
answer a week beats ten spammy ones.

### 8. A light YouTube presence with clean transcripts
YouTube mentions were the single strongest AI-visibility correlate in the research. Even short, plain
screen-recordings ("how to tell if a Meta ad is fatiguing") with a written transcript/description create
citable, trusted content. Longer-horizon, but high-value.

---

## Tier 3 — Consistency (the multiplier under everything)

Google's June 2025 Knowledge Graph update *removed* billions of poorly-corroborated entities. Inconsistency
reads as ambiguity and blocks a Knowledge Panel. So, everywhere you appear, use the **same**:
- **Name:** "AdBrain AI" (pick one and never vary it).
- **One-line description:** "AdBrain reads your Meta and Google ad accounts and tells you what to scale,
  refresh, or kill, with a reason for every call."
- **Website + logo.**
Run a quarterly audit of every profile for drift.

> Note on the brand↔domain mismatch: the site is on **adscaledigital.co** but the brand and emails are
> **AdBrain**. This split weakens the entity signal. Deciding whether to move to a real adbrain.* domain (with
> 301 redirects) is a business call, but it would materially strengthen everything above.

---

## What NOT to do (these cause exclusion, not citation)
- No fake or incentivized reviews (violates the review sites' terms and Google's policy).
- No bought/PBN backlinks or link schemes.
- No coordinated/astroturfed Reddit/forum posting.
- No `llms.txt` effort — AI bots don't read it (~97% never fetched); it is not a real signal.
- Don't chase word count or stuff pages with stats/authority language — over-optimized pages tested *worse*.

---

## 30 / 60 / 90 day sequence
- **Days 1-30:** Tier 1 in full (listings, Wikidata, alerts) + set up HARO/Connectively. Lock the name +
  one-liner (Tier 3).
- **Days 31-60:** 2-3 HARO answers/week; pitch 3 podcasts; get onto 2 "best-of" lists; start 1 Reddit answer/week.
- **Days 61-90:** first 2-3 mentions should be landing; reclaim any unlinked ones; publish 1 comparison article;
  first YouTube clips.

## How to know it's working
- **Google Search Console** (set this up first) — impressions/clicks over time.
- **Brand search volume** for "AdBrain" (a rising branded-search trend is a leading indicator of entity strength).
- **Count of third-party pages** that mention AdBrain (track monthly).
- **Spot-check AI answers:** ask ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini a question in your space and see if/when AdBrain
  starts appearing. Expect months, not weeks.

---

*This is a build-the-moat play, not a this-quarter traffic play. AI referral traffic is still ~1% of total for
most sites today, but it converts far better and the competition for citations is still thin. The brands that
start now own the answer surface later.*
