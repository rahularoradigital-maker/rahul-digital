You're winning on deal-led scale and spec-stack dominance, making boAt feel like the safest high-feature buy when price is the trigger. Where You Lead

Deal-led spec authority
You consistently out-muscle the set with a repeatable combo of category leadership ("India's #1", "King of ANC") plus concrete feature anchors (60–150h battery, ASAP charge, Spatial/Dolby, 700W/STARVIS 4K) and aggressive offer stacks (60–70% off, coupons, extra site %). That system converts price-intent fast and reinforces "buy official = legit" as a low-friction default.

Where You Lag

Trustable premium proof
JBL turns "premium" into reduced risk with tangible reassurance (80-year heritage, buy from JBL.com for an extra 12 months warranty) and a clear performance narrative ("blocks the chaos", 6-mic calls), while your performance moment and proof of "pro-grade" quality are thinner. This leaves you exposed to the belief that boAt is "only budget" and that ANC/battery claims are just marketing—especially when the discount lever isn't the headline.

What To Do

Own real-life outcomes
Shift your hero creative from price-first to scenario-first proof (commute calls, office focus, gym, travel) with one measurable claim per ad (e.g., mic count/noise cancellation, battery use-case, ANC level) and a simple authenticity/reassurance layer to match JBL's risk reduction. Keep offers as the closer, not the opener, so proof survives beyond sale windows and competes with realme's lifestyle battery story and OnePlus's festival attention grabs.
How we read "conviction": Meta Ad Library doesn't expose ROAS, CTR or spend. We infer brand conviction from ad volume, ad-age (running-since), multi-placement, creator-page amplification, and repetition of the same creative pattern. A pattern that's old + replicated + multi-placement + creator-amplified is one the brand is doubling down on.
