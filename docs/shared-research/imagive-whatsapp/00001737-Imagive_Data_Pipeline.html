01 · boAt in 30 seconds

The brand we're solving for.

boAt makes audio for young India. Earbuds, headphones, speakers, smartwatches. Price band ₹599 to ₹6,000. Their customer is a 22-year-old in any Indian city — cricket fan, Spotify on the metro, scrolls Instagram all day, paying attention to price.

Their ad style
  • Fast-cut UGC, vertical 9:16
  • Cricket + IPL references
  • Founder Aman Gupta visible
  • Price always on screen
  • Mostly under 15 seconds
Who we benchmark against
  • Noise — smartwatch-led, cleaner tech look
  • Boult — value-led, less brand swagger
  • pTron — budget floor (₹299–₹999)
  • Realme Audio — companion to Realme phones
02 · 5 small things we do on day 1

These make us ready for 2028 without rebuilding later.

Five 1-line decisions in Week 1. Add maybe 5% effort up front. Save us 6 months of rework in year 2.

1
Every ad gets a concept_id.Ads come in families. We track which family each one belongs to.
2
Every ad gets a parent_id.Which earlier ad inspired this one. Family tree starts forming on day 1.
3
Every ad gets a platform_id.Meta today. TikTok and YouTube tomorrow. Same database.
4
Every ad has a federated_ok flag.Can this row contribute to anonymous network learning later? Default yes for patterns, no for raw files.
5
Every output carries receipts.Every recommendation must link back to the source ads it learned from. No exceptions.
PHASE 1 Weeks 1–3 · Sprint 1

Pull every ad. Tag every ad. Pull out what's inside.

We scrape boAt's full Meta Ad Library and the libraries of Noise, Boult, pTron and Realme Audio. Every ad gets stored with the 5 foundations from day 1. Then we label each ad (UGC vs studio, problem hook vs feature hook, etc.) and break it open to extract the 14 layers — hook line, voice tone, environment, on-screen text timing, all of it. By end of Week 3, every ad is a clean row in the database.

IN5 brand URLs from Meta Ad Library + a daily scraper.
OUT~600–1,200 ads stored, each with 14 layers + bucket tags + lifecycle stage.
WHYNothing else works without this. Get the shape right now, every later feature is just one more column.
▴ 2028-ready: the scraper interface is abstract. Plug in TikTok or YouTube libraries later — no database changes.
PHASE 2 Weeks 4–6 · Sprint 2

Compare boAt with the category. Find the gaps.

Now we use the data. We build the DNA scorecard — boAt's creative fingerprint next to all 4 competitors on one page. Then the whitespace map — which hook + format combinations are empty in the category. Last, the variety score — is boAt putting all their eggs in one basket?

boAt example. Likely gap surfaced: boAt is 60% problem-led hooks (cricket-themed UGC), 0% transformation-led. Noise is 70% transformation. The map says — "Test transformation + founder POV with Aman Gupta. Nobody else owns that cell yet."

INThe 14-layer data from Phase 1 + boAt's persona and product list.
OUTDNA scorecard, whitespace map, variety score, top 3 gap recommendations — each with source ads attached.
WHYThis is the "ah-ha" screen. The marketer sees the gap, clicks any number to see the proof, and trusts the tool. Without this, we don't earn the right to give advice.
PHASE 3 Week 7 · Sprint 3

Connect boAt's Ads Manager. Get the real numbers.

Up to here, "best ads" is a guess based on how long the ad lived and how many versions it has. Once boAt connects their Business Manager, the guesses become facts. We pull CTR, hook rate, hold rate, frequency, CPA, ROAS — daily — for every live ad.

boAt example. The data probably says: top 20% by ROAS are UGC handheld with a cricket reference and on-screen price by second 1. Bottom 20% are slow-hook studio shoots. We can now say "stop making the bottom 20% style" with confidence.

INboAt's Meta BM access + the 14-layer data from Phase 1.
OUTOne row per ad with creative DNA + real performance. The "best of boAt" and "worst of boAt" lists are now defensible.
WHYThis is the truth layer. It's the difference between "we think this works" and "this gives you 4× ROAS, the receipts are right here."
▴ 2028-ready: the connector is built abstract. Add TikTok Business, YouTube Ads, Google Ads later without rebuilding the schema.
PHASE 4 Weeks 8–9 · Sprint 3

Predict fatigue 5–7 days before CTR shows it.

Most teams catch fatigue when CTR drops. By then a week of budget is gone. We catch it earlier by combining two signals: what's happening inside (hook rate decay, frequency creep, CPA creep, hold rate falling) and what's happening outside (competitors refreshing, killing ads, launching waves). Together: 5–7 day early warning. Plus, for every dying ad, we pre-stage 3 replacements — hook swap, new shoot, full pivot.

boAt example. Day 0 — Airdopes ad #bA0042 hook rate falls from 8% to 6.5%. Day 1 — Noise drops 5 new transformation ads. Imagive's call: "ad #bA0042 will hit critical fatigue in ~6 days. 3 replacements ready. Want me to launch all 3 at 10% budget each?"

INDaily performance from Phase 3 + competitor lifecycle behavior from Phase 1.
OUTFatigue radar — every live ad ranked by days-to-critical, with 3 replacement concepts pre-staged.
WHYThis is the most loved feature for performance marketers. Catching fatigue at week N-1 instead of week N = smooth replacement instead of panic.
▴ 2028-ready: the model takes 4 signal sources. Only 2 are turned on today. Sources 3 (brand-learned) and 4 (federated network) are stubbed in code. Switch them on in 2027 — no model rewrite.
PHASE 5 Week 10 · Sprint 4

Click "Generate 10" → 10 finished ad concepts come out.

The action layer. boAt sees the gap. boAt clicks one button. In 45–90 seconds, 10 ad concepts appear — statics, UGC scripts, or carousels. Each one has a generated hero image, a hook line, 3 copy variations, on-screen text timed beat-by-beat, and the source competitor ads it learned from.

boAt example. One concept from the batch: transformation × UGC × founder POV. Hook: "I bought 3 earphones in 6 months. Then I tried boAt." Bedroom desk visual. 3 copy variations. Sources: Noise #N4521 (84d, 12 variants) + Realme #R0814 (67d, 5 variants) + boAt's own UGC winners.

INThe identified gap + boAt's DNA + top 3 reference ads + current offers.
OUT10 ready-to-ship concepts in 45–90 seconds, each with full source receipts.
WHYEvery other AI ad tool stops at "here's an idea." We stop at "here's the finished concept, ready for your designer." That's the moat.
PHASE 6 Weeks 11–12 · Sprint 4

Close the loop. Send the Friday Brief. Ship.

Two things in two weeks. First, we wire the learning loop. Every shipped concept's actual ROAS flows back. The brand-specific brain starts dumb but gets smarter every week. Second, we automate the Friday Brief — every Friday morning the customer gets a one-pager with risks, gaps, 5 briefs, 1 hypothesis. Plus a "generate 10 to close this week's top gap" button at the bottom.

boAt example by Week 12. The brain has learned: "founder POV ads beat your median ROAS by 1.6×. UGC handheld with on-screen text beats by 1.3×. Studio polish underperforms by 0.7×." That memory keeps growing every week we run.

INEvery shipped concept's real performance + a weekly schedule.
OUTA learning brain per brand + a Friday email habit + a live product.
WHYThe Friday Brief turns the tool into a habit. The learning loop is the moat — most "AI ad tools" generate, the user ships, the data never comes back. We close that loop from day 1.
▴ 2028-ready: the federated layer (anonymous patterns across all Imagive brands) has its slot reserved in the loop. When we cross ~10 brands, we flip it on. The 11th brand makes the 1st brand smarter.
