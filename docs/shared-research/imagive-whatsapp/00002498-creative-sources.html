Two rules that cover all five elements

Hero elements (hook + product) are the composition: the product fills the frame as a single in-use hero (~55%), the hook is the largest text in the top third (~20%) — no boxes, contrast carries them. Copy & offer elements (CTA, price, offer) are small self-contained badges/buttons in clear space — never a box around the whole ad, never over the product (Meta flags overlapping auto-badges as a failure mode).

Compiled to justify the creative rules in HookGeneratorController::generateImage. Hero elements are applied in IMAGE_PROMPT_SYSTEM: product = single in-use hero filling the frame (~55%) · hook = largest text, top third, single headline. Copy & offer elements are applied in $renderRules: CTA as a high-contrast button in the bottom band · price as a small tag/badge · offer as a bold badge · original price struck through beside the sale price — all in clear space, never over the product, and stepped aside when a style reference drives the layout.
