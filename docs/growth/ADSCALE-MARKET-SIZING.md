# AdScale — Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)

_Aug 2026, live web research. **Estimates, clearly labelled** — triangulated top-down + bottom-up. Treat as a
decision model with confidence levels, not audited figures. Product: AdScale (adscaledigital.co) — subscription
SaaS "creative decision intelligence" for DTC/ecommerce brands + agencies running Meta + Google ads._

---

## Market definition

- **Problem space:** ad teams running meaningful Meta/Google spend can't tell, fast and with confidence, what
  to change today — Ads Manager overwhelms and dashboards don't *decide*.
- **Buyers:** in-house performance marketers at DTC/ecommerce brands, and agencies running client ad accounts.
- **Value metric:** annual subscription revenue (per-account / per-seat), self-serve to mid-market.
- **Scope:** value-based (revenue) sizing. Global TAM; **US-first** SAM (English, Meta+Google, self-serve tier).
- **Threshold:** brands spending roughly **$5k+/mo** on ads — below that, a decision tool doesn't pay for
  itself (competitors converge on this: Triple Whale "genuinely useful $15k+/mo"; Motion "$150–500/mo worth it
  at meaningful spend").

## TAM — Total Addressable Market

**Top-down.** The **marketing-analytics software market ≈ $6.5–8.0B in 2026**, growing **~14% CAGR** toward
~$18B by 2033. AdScale sits in the **ad-decision / creative-analytics-for-ecommerce** slice of that — call it
**~25–35%** of the analytics market (the rest is web/CRM/attribution/BI). → **TAM ≈ $1.8–2.5B (2026).**

**Bottom-up (cross-check).** Global businesses running meaningful Meta/Google ads that would pay for a decision
layer: US ecommerce advertisers on Meta alone ≈ **142k**; total Meta advertisers ≈ **1.48M** (a fraction serious
+ ecommerce). Estimate **~800k–1.1M** global serious DTC/ecommerce advertisers + agencies at the $5k+/mo
threshold. Blended ACV **≈ $2,400/yr** (from real competitor pricing: Motion $150–500/mo, Madgicx $49–499/mo,
Triple Whale $179–1,849/mo, Atria $159–329/mo). → **1M × $2,400 ≈ $2.4B TAM.**

**Reconciliation:** top-down ($1.8–2.5B) and bottom-up ($2.4B) converge. **TAM ≈ $2.0–2.5B, growing ~14%/yr.**

## SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

**Constraints that narrow TAM to what AdScale can realistically serve today:**
- **US-first** (English UI, US GTM) → the US is ~30–40% of the serious-advertiser base.
- **Meta + Google** focus (not TikTok-primary creative teams).
- **Self-serve to mid-market** ($5k–$500k/mo spend) — not enterprise custom, not sub-$5k.

US serious DTC/ecommerce advertisers + SMB/mid-market agencies ≈ **~180–230k** businesses. At ~$2,400 ACV →
**SAM ≈ $430–550M** — roughly **~20–22% of TAM**. **Use SAM ≈ $0.45–0.5B.**

## SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (1–3 years)

**Reality check:** AdScale is **pre-launch, solo-built, one product, no brand — and carries a name collision**
with an established competitor (adscale.com). Competitors are entrenched (Motion 2,100+ teams; Triple Whale
50k+ brands; Madgicx, Atria). A new entrant captures a **tiny** share initially, won on a sharp wedge
("decide, and prove why"), not breadth.

**Bottom-up SOM:** a focused new entrant reaching **~500–2,000 paying customers** at ~$2,400 ACV in 3 years →
**~$1.2M–$4.8M ARR**. That is **~0.3–1.0% of SAM** — the right order of magnitude for year 1–3 of a
differentiated niche SaaS. **Base case SOM ≈ $1–3M ARR by year 3; upside ~$5M** if the agency motion + the
reason-first wedge land.

## Growth drivers & trends

- **Meta projected to pass Google as #1 digital ad platform in 2026** — creative + Meta-decision tooling gets
  more valuable; tailwind for AdScale's Meta-first strength.
- **First-party data + signal loss** → brands need a decision layer that reads their *own* day-wise data.
- **AI-in-ads maturation** → buyers want AI that *explains*, not another black-box autopilot (AdScale's wedge).
- **Creative fatigue accelerating** → more decisions/day per buyer → higher willingness to pay for speed.
- **Agency consolidation** → a multi-account motion multiplies ACV and lowers CAC (built-in advocacy).
- **Contracting forces:** platform-native tooling improving (Advantage+), competitors adding verdict layers,
  ad-tool fatigue / budget scrutiny in a tight market.

## Key assumptions & risks (numbered, with confidence)

1. **Blended ACV ≈ $2,400/yr.** _Medium._ Grounded in real competitor pricing, but AdScale's own price is
   unset. Validate: price-test 2–3 tiers against 20 target buyers. **Most material single lever on SOM.**
2. **~1M global serious advertisers at the $5k+/mo threshold.** _Medium._ Extrapolated from US Meta ecommerce
   advertiser counts. Validate: Meta Ad Library + Shopify tier data + analyst reports.
3. **Ad-decision slice = ~25–35% of the marketing-analytics market.** _Low–Medium._ A scoping judgment.
   Validate against a narrower "ad analytics / creative analytics" analyst segment if one exists.
4. **US = ~30–40% of the serious base (SAM scoping).** _Medium._ Standard US-share heuristic for B2B SaaS.
5. **SOM 0.3–1% of SAM in 3 years.** _Medium._ Typical for a differentiated new entrant; **highly sensitive to
   the name collision** (assumption: it is resolved — if not, CAC rises and SOM compresses).
6. **Willingness to pay for a *decision* layer over free Ads Manager.** _Low–Medium._ The core bet. Validate
   with the journey-map's "aha" test: does a correct, explained, money-saving verdict convert trial → paid?

**What would materially change the estimates:** (a) pricing (ACV) — ±2× swings SOM directly; (b) resolving vs
keeping the name (CAC + brand SEO); (c) moving up-market to agencies/enterprise (raises ACV, changes SAM); (d)
a competitor shipping "explained verdicts" first (narrows the wedge).

**Sharpen with:** Meta Ad Library advertiser pulls, a Shopify-tier revenue distribution report, a dedicated
"ad analytics" analyst segment, and 15–20 buyer price-sensitivity interviews.
