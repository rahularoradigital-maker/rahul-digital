# AdScale — Value Proposition Statements

_For marketing, sales, and onboarding. Grounded in the real product and the "decide, and prove why" wedge — no
fabricated results, no hype. Voice: plain, confident, specific; **shows its work**. (Product = AdScale /
adscaledigital.co — decision intelligence for Meta + Google ads.)_

## Core positioning statement

> **AdScale is the ad decision engine that shows its work.** It reads your Meta and Google accounts day by day
> and tells you the two or three changes that will actually move revenue today — with the evidence, the
> agreement of the signals, and a confidence level behind every call. It never touches your account. You decide;
> it proves why.

**One-liner:** _Know what to change today, and be able to defend it — without handing a black box the keys._

---

## Segment value-proposition statements

### 1. For the in-house performance marketer (defends decisions to a VP)
AdScale gives performance marketers a defensible answer to "what do I do today, and why?" — every morning,
in five minutes. Instead of tabbing between Ads Manager and a spreadsheet you don't fully trust, you get a
ranked "what to act on" list, each call carrying a **triple-labelled verdict** (is it even judgeable? do the
signals agree? how confident?) you can take straight to your boss. And because AdScale applies real buyer
rigor — it won't call fatigue on an ad that barely spent — you stop acting on noise and start defending every
move with evidence.

### 2. For the DTC founder / growth lead (no time to read the account daily)
AdScale watches your ad spend so you don't have to. It reads your Meta and Google data every day and surfaces
where money is leaking and what to change — ranked by rupees at stake, and only on ads that are actually
running. No new dashboard to learn, no black-box autopilot changing your account behind your back: you get a
short, honest read of what's working, what's wasting, and what to do, so you can spend your time on the
business instead of the ad account.

### 3. For the agency media buyer / owner (many client accounts, defensible reporting)
AdScale gives agencies the same rigorous, explainable read across every client account — so a junior buyer
reads an account like a 20-year veteran, and every recommendation comes with a reason a client will respect.
Each verdict is traceable to a rule and a confidence level, which turns your monthly reporting from "trust me"
into "here's the evidence." Add accounts as you win them; the read is consistent, fast, and never auto-changes
a client's campaigns without your sign-off.

### 4. For the creative strategist (which creative to refresh next)
AdScale tells creative teams which ad is genuinely wearing out — and which just looks like it is. It reads
day-wise thumb-stop, hold, and click-to-checkout at the ad, ad-set, and campaign level, and separates real
creative fatigue from statistical noise (it won't flag an ad that spent too little to judge). So you refresh
the creatives that actually need it, back it with evidence, and stop wasting production cycles on ads that were
never the problem.

## Context variants

### Marketing (website / social / ads)
- **Headline:** _The ad decision engine that shows its work._
- **Sub:** _Reads your Meta + Google accounts daily. Tells you what to change today — with the reason and the
  confidence. Never touches your account._
- **Against the category:** _Not another dashboard. Not an autopilot. The tool that decides, and proves why._
- **For the "burned by a black box" buyer:** _Tired of AI that just says "pause this"? AdScale tells you why,
  how sure it is, and leaves the decision to you._

### Sales (pitch / deck)
- **The problem line:** _"Your VP asks why ROAS dropped every Monday. Ads Manager won't tell you; a dashboard
  won't decide. AdScale gives you the answer — and the evidence to defend it."_
- **The differentiator line:** _"Everyone else either grades your creatives or auto-changes your account.
  AdScale is the only one that gives you a decision *with a reason you can defend* — and never touches the
  account without you."_
- **The rigor proof:** _"We won't call fatigue on an ad that spent under 20% of its ad set's budget — because
  that's noise, not signal. That single rule is why buyers trust the verdicts."_

### Onboarding (first-run / activation — engineer the "aha")
- **Connect step:** _Connect your Meta account — read-only, encrypted, and we never change a thing. This takes
  30 seconds; your first read is a minute away._
- **First verdict:** _Here's the first thing worth your attention today — and exactly why we flagged it.
  Evidence, signal agreement, and how confident we are. If we're not sure, we'll say so._
- **The aha nudge:** _See that ad we marked "not judgeable, spent only 4% of its ad set"? A dashboard would've
  told you to kill it. That's the difference — we only call what's real._

## Voice guardrails (keep every statement on-brand)

**Use:** plain Indian/US English, specific numbers only when real, "shows its work," "decide," "defend,"
"evidence," "confidence," "never touches your account," "what to do today."

**Avoid:** "revolutionize," "unleash," "seamless," "next-gen," "game-changer," "AI-powered" as a crutch,
fabricated ROAS/results, fake testimonials, exclamation-mark hype, "10x your ads." (Confidence, not loudness.)

**Never claim:** a specific ROAS lift, guaranteed results, or a case study that hasn't happened. The credibility
IS the product — don't undercut it with a claim you can't defend. That would break the very promise ("shows its
work") the whole brand rests on.
