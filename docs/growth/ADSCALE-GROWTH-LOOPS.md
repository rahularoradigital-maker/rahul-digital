# AdScale — Growth Loops

_How AdScale compounds acquisition through product-native loops, not just paid/founder-led outreach. Grounded
in the real product (a decision tool for ad teams), the ICP (Maya, the performance marketer who must defend
calls to a VP), and the strategy trilogy (journey map, competitor analysis, sizing)._

## Product value (the loop's fuel)

- **Primary action:** connect account → get a triple-labelled verdict ("what to do today, and why") → act.
- **Value per action:** a correct, defensible, money-saving decision the buyer can take to their VP.
- **Network effects today:** ~none (single-user utility). The loops must *manufacture* them.
- **The advocacy trigger (from the journey map):** _"it makes me look smart to my boss / peers."_ That is the
  single most powerful, product-native sharing motive AdScale has.

## The honest constraint (killcritic — read first)

AdScale operates on a **confidential, competitive workflow** (a brand's ad performance). Two dampeners most
growth-loop playbooks ignore:
1. **Confidentiality:** buyers will NOT publicly post their real account numbers. Any shareable artifact must
   be **anonymized/generic by default** ("AdScale caught a ₹X wasted-spend leak") or opt-in.
2. **Edge-hoarding:** some buyers see AdScale as *their* advantage and won't hand peers the tool. Frame sharing
   as **status** ("look what I caught"), not "here's my edge — go get it." Design for the sharer's ego, not the
   product's reach.

Consequence: **B2B utility viral is an accelerant, not an exponential engine.** It multiplies the content/SEO/
community top-of-funnel (the growth agent) — it doesn't replace it.

## The 5 loops, ranked for AdScale

| Rank | Loop | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Viral (shareable verdict/insight card)** | **Strong** | The "makes me look smart" artifact — the strongest B2B sharing motive; feeds the community top-of-funnel. |
| **2** | **Collaboration ("invite your boss" + agency multi-account)** | **Strong** | The VP is the *buyer*; agencies expand to N client accounts. Drives retention + expansion, not just reach. |
| 3 | **Referral (give/get a free month)** | Medium | Easy to add, measurable; agencies refer peers. Not a moat. |
| 4 | Usage (product output shared publicly) | Medium | Overlaps the viral loop; same artifact. |
| 5 | User-generated (discover others' content → create) | Weak | No content network; far off. Skip for now. |

## Recommended PRIMARY loop — the shareable "caught it" card (viral)

**Mechanism:** AdScale surfaces a non-obvious, defensible catch (the journey-map "aha" + the monthly "it caught
what you'd have missed" moment) → buyer taps **"Share this"** → AdScale generates a clean, credible,
**anonymized-by-default** card ("_AdScale flagged a wasted-spend leak this account would've missed — with the
reasoning_") + a subtle attributed CTA ("_read your own account free_") → posted to **LinkedIn/Slack/X** →
a peer sees it → lands on adscaledigital.co → connects → gets their own aha. Repeats on the next catch.

- **Trigger:** a high-confidence, surprising verdict (gate on the triple label — only share what's defensible).
- **Incentive:** intrinsic — status/credibility. No reward needed (and rewards would cheapen it).
- **Share friction:** one tap → a ready image + prefilled caption. Confidentiality handled by anonymizing.
- **Repeat:** monthly guaranteed catch + ad-hoc big ones.

**Why primary:** it's the only loop that rides AdScale's actual advocacy motive, and every shared card is
also **content for the growth agent** (repurpose into posts/guides) — the two systems compound.

## SECONDARY loop — "invite your boss" + agency accounts (collaboration)

- **Invite the VP:** the person Maya defends decisions to is the *buyer*. In the verdict view: "**Share this
  read-only with your VP**" → the VP sees the reasoning + confidence → becomes champion/buyer. Near-100% of
  serious users will eventually loop in their boss; that's a high-conversion, product-native invite.
- **Agency multi-account:** an agency connects account #2, #3… #15 — each is expansion revenue AND the agency
  evangelizes AdScale to clients (built-in advocacy, the highest-LTV motion from the sizing doc).

## TERTIARY loop — referral (layer later)

Give-a-month/get-a-month, targeted at **agencies referring agencies** (tight peer networks). Simple, but add it
only after the viral + collaboration loops are instrumented — don't lead with an incentive that attracts
low-intent signups.

## Loop coefficient (honest, order-of-magnitude)

**Viral (standalone):** ~20% of active users share ~1 card/month × ~50 relevant peer impressions × ~1.5% CTR ×
~15% connect ≈ **K ≈ 0.02 new user / user / month** from viral alone — *slow* (B2B utility viral always is).
**Collaboration:** invite rate ≈ 0.7 boss/teammate per active user (one-time), high activation — a strong
*seeding* multiplier, not a compounding K. **Blended:** the loops make paid/community CAC ~20–40% cheaper and
seed the top-of-funnel; they are **accelerants on the content engine, not a viral rocket.** Model them as CAC
reduction + assisted acquisition, not standalone exponential growth.

## Metrics framework (measure weekly)

- **Viral:** catches surfaced → share rate → cards created → card impressions → card CTR → visit→connect →
  connect→activate → **viral K**.
- **Collaboration:** invite rate (boss/teammate), invite→active, agency accounts/logo, seat/account expansion.
- **Referral:** referral send rate, referral→paid, incentive cost per acquired customer.
- **Guardrails:** % shares anonymized (confidentiality), unsubscribe/complaint rate, share→churn (edge-hoarding
  backlash), CAC with vs without loops.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day roadmap

- **30 days:** instrument the "aha" + monthly-catch moment; build the **one-tap anonymized shareable verdict
  card** (image + attributed CTA); add **"share read-only with your VP"** to the verdict view. Baseline share
  + invite rates.
- **60 days:** ship the **agency multi-account invite** flow; launch a **narrow referral** (agency→agency);
  wire shared cards into the **growth agent's content engine** (repurpose catches into posts/guides).
- **90 days:** optimize card format for **LinkedIn** (the buyer's platform); A/B captions/CTAs; measure blended
  K + CAC impact; decide which loop to double down on.

## Priorities (start with ONE)

1. **Build the shareable "caught it" card first** — it's the highest-leverage, rides the real advocacy motive,
   and doubles as content-engine fuel.
2. **Then "invite your boss"** — it converts the buyer and lifts retention.
3. **Referral last**, and only agency-targeted.
_Do not build all three at once. Master the card loop, then layer._
