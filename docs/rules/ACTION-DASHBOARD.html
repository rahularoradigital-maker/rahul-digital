how it thinksReading 90 days, day by day

Every recommendation on this page comes from reading the account day by day, not as a single average.

The tool pulls the last 60 to 90 days at time_increment=1 (one row per creative per day), so it sees the slope of every metric, not just today's value. A single average hides a creative that is dying. A day-wise series shows the decay, the weekday pattern, the festival spike, and the exact day a trend will cross a line.

From that day-wise history it learns four things: which creative elements win and how fast they fatigue, the account's own healthy ranges (never a public benchmark), the seasonal shape, and, where useful, the gaps against competitors read from the public ad library. Those four feed the next steps and the new concepts below.

what to make nextNew concepts to shoot this week

Each concept is a buildable recipe, and every part is chosen from four sources: our own winners, the AI category model, competitor ads, and the world norm. Nothing is a guess, every piece traces to a number. From here it hands off on its own: the recipe goes to Claude for the script and image prompt, then to nano-banana for the visuals with every asset attached, then to the Media Team to approve.
recipe = pick(SKU) + pick(format) + pick(concept) + pick(offer) + pick(landing) each pick = argmax over candidates of: 0.40 x our_performance (day-wise, what already wins for us) + 0.25 x ai_fit (AdBrain category model) + 0.20 x competitor_gap (whitespace vs the category peer set: your country + category + products within +/- 30% of your price) + 0.15 x world_norm (category benchmark) constraints: SKU in stock and margin healthy · concept fills the top funnel or message gap format = our best-winning for that stage · offer protects margin · landing fixes the weakest funnel step Source tags on each part: OURS = our data · AI = model · COMP = competitor · WORLD = norm · CRYSTAL = decode taxonomy
