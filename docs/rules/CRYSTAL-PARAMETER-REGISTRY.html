Everything AdBrain captures: crystals, parameters and the KPIs that matter most

The full list of what the system reads from every ad and every account, with the rule behind each, the logic (why it matters), the context, and the use case (the action it drives). Numbers are canon, from 03_BUSINESS_RULES.md and 06_CRYSTALS_MASTER.xlsx. Money as Rs or USD. No em dashes.

HOW TO READ THISFour words, defined once

Crystal. One family of things the AI tags on a creative. There are 30 crystals, in 12 layers (A to L). Together they are the decode taxonomy, the DNA of an ad.
Sub-signal. One exact thing tagged inside a crystal. There are 167 sub-signals across the 30 crystals. Example: inside "Sound design fingerprint", one sub-signal is "sound-off comprehension score".
Parameter. A number the scoring engines compute from the crystals and the account data. Example: DiversityScore, FatigueScore, duplicate similarity.
KPI. A business number the Expert and the CMO act on. Example: blended MER, CAC, add-to-cart rate. The most important ones are in Part C.
One naming point, so nobody is confused. The dashboard first fold shows a "crystals score" like 57 of 70, which is a simple health rollup for the CMO. That 70 is a count of health checks. The canonical crystals here are the 30 decode crystals that describe a creative. The five analytics reads on the dashboard (account, creative, competitor, diversity, fatigue) are the engines in Part B that turn these crystals and the account data into scores.

This is what the AI tags on every image and video ad. Layers A to L, from surface facts to deep meaning. Phase and priority are canon. Table stakes and Must-ship are the Phase-1 build; High leverage and Nice to have are Phase 2 and 3.

PART BThe scoring engines and parameters, with canon formulas

The engines turn the crystals and the account data into scores. Every weight and threshold here is canon from 03_BUSINESS_RULES.md. All weights are editable in the Measurement Canon; these are the defaults.

1. Diversity score (8 dimensions, weights sum to 1.00)

DiversityScore = SUM(weight_i x score_i) Hook 0.20 | Angle 0.20 | Persona 0.15 | Funnel 0.15 | Format 0.10 | Offer 0.08 | Innovation 0.06 | AntiRedundancy 0.06 HookScore = 50 x Distinct + 30 x Balance + 20 x Novelty (12 unique hooks in 30d = full) AngleScore = 40 x ClusterCount + 40 x Balance + 20 x Coverage (12 canonical angles) PersonaScore= 50 x CoverageFraction + 50 x (1 - PersonaHHI) FunnelScore = 100 x (1 - JSD(actual, [0.50, 0.25, 0.25])) (ideal TOF/MOF/BOF)
Rule12 canonical angles: product_demo, founder_story, ugc_review, celebrity_drop, bass_test, comparison, problem_solution, festive_sale, new_launch, nostalgia, unboxing, lifestyle_montage. Benchmarks: 68 average, top quartile 78 to 82.
LogicMeta treats near-copies as one option, so variety across hook, angle, persona and funnel is what gives the algorithm real choices. Ideal funnel mix is 50/25/25.
ContextComputed on the ad library you supplied, not the spend Meta allocates, because Meta concentrates budget on purpose.
Use caseThe diversity read on the dashboard, and the whitespace that briefs the next concept. Loop stops when DiversityScore is at or above 80.

2. Fatigue score and half-life

FatigueScore = 100 x (0.5 x CTR_decay + 0.3 x Frequency_ratio(freq/5) + 0.2 x Engagement_decay) Tiers: Fresh 0-30 | Healthy 31-50 | Tiring 51-70 | Burnt 71-100
RuleAuto-pause when score is above 70 AND confidence is above 85 percent. Max 20 pauses a day. 3 replacement creatives required before a pause runs.
LogicFatigue is really audience saturation. Built on decay in CTR and engagement plus frequency, not a single frequency threshold.
ContextPredictive signals: CTR down 20 percent versus the 7-day average, CPM up 25 percent versus 30 days, frequency above 4, a 7 to 14 day burnout forecast, a 60-day competitor-copying window.
Use caseThe fatigue read, the "what will break and when" warning, and the stop list on the action board.

3. Duplication and opportunity

sim(A,B) = 0.6 x cosine(vision_embed) + 0.4 x cosine(transcript_embed) cluster if sim >= 0.92 | waste flag at >= 4 near-duplicates OpportunityScore = 0.45 x RevenueImpact + 0.25 x ScalePotential + 0.15 x EaseOfExecution + 0.15 x Confidence
RuleTwo ads are the same concept when similarity is 0.92 or higher. Four or more near-duplicates raise a waste flag.
LogicNineteen thumbnails can be twelve real options in Meta's eye. Distinctness is measured to the machine, not the human eye.
Use caseThe cluster view, the "stop 10 of 11 copies" call, and ranking which new concept to shoot next.

4. Brand search hygiene (the 99/0/0 rule)

Healthy brand campaign: >= 99% spend on true brand terms, Rs 0 on competitor terms, Rs 0 on generic impact_score = spend x confidence x log(1 + clicks)
RuleThe old 90/10/10 health rule is dead. A campaign is confirmed as a brand campaign at 90 percent brand spend, but a healthy one is 99/0/0. Fuzzy matching for brand and competitor only (edit distance 2), never generic.
LogicMoney on generic or competitor terms inside a brand campaign is leakage dressed as brand performance.
Use caseLeakage and cannibalisation findings, ranked by impact. An ambassador name alone is not brand ("KL Rahul" alone is a cricketer search).

5. Confidence gates

Auto-accept classification: >= 97% (under 97: retry stronger model, then Expert UNKNOWN queue) Operational auto-action gate: > 85% Onboarding: >= 90 confirm-all | 70-90 review cards | < 70 manual
RuleNothing is auto-accepted below 97 percent confidence on any AI classification. Auto-actions need above 85 percent.
LogicA wrong label poisons every score built on it, so the floor is high and the uncertain cases go to the Expert.
Use caseThe routing between the AI layer and the Expert, and the confidence shown on every tile.

6. Operational thresholds (the account triggers)

Anomaly: z-score > 2 sigma vs 14-day baseline Pacing healthy: 85-115% of expected spend Quality Score alarm: -2 points in 7 days Brand impression share alarm: -10% Placement audit: > 500 impressions AND ROAS < half account average Winner boost: +20% budget Bid changes: +/- 15% per day Channel rebalance: <= 20% CAPI dedup: > 95% required Out-of-stock auto-pause: within ~4 minutes LTV:CAC target: 3.5x
RuleThese are the exact numbers that fire an alert or an action across the account engines.
LogicEach ties to a cause, not a vibe. Scaling is capped so a winner boost does not trigger a learning reset.
Use caseThe early warnings, the money-on-the-table panel, and the change-history checks.

RECONCILEHow the crystals, the engines and the KPIs fit

The 30 crystals in Part A are what the AI reads off each ad. The engines in Part B turn those crystals plus the account data into scores like diversity and fatigue. The KPIs in Part C are the business numbers the Expert and the CMO act on. The dashboard's five reads (account, creative, competitor, diversity, fatigue) are the engines, and the first-fold "crystals score" is a simple health rollup of how many checks are green. Nothing here is a separate product, it is one chain: crystal to score to KPI to decision, and every step shows its working.
