Section 1How the two tiers work together

This is the whole idea in one picture. Tier 2 describes. Tier 1 decides. And Tier 1 is literally built out of Tier 2, plus one hard thing a platform will never hand you.

Tier 2 · easy to get

The raw dials

Straight from Meta, Google or Shopify. One API call, or one division of two numbers you already have. A junior can pull them daily.

They tell you what is happening. CPM went up. Hook rate fell. Add-to-cart rate is 2 percent. All true, all fast, and none of them tell you if you are actually making money.

Tier 1 · hard to get and calculate

The truth metrics

Each one is built from several Tier 2 numbers, plus one hard ingredient: the finance sheet (COGS), a customer join (new vs returning), an ad decode, or a holdout test.

They tell you what to do. Contribution ROAS says if growth adds profit. nCAC says the true cost to grow. Half-life says when a creative dies. These decide, and they are exactly the ones most brands cannot produce cleanly.

The rule that connects them

You cannot compute a Tier 1 metric without its Tier 2 inputs plus its one hard ingredient. So the build order is fixed: capture every Tier 2 dial first, then add the four hard ingredients (finance sheet, customer join, decoder, holdout), and only then do the Tier 1 truth metrics light up. Miss one hard ingredient and a whole row of Tier 1 stays dark. The table below is the wiring diagram.

Say it to a 10 year old

Tier 2 is like the speed shown on a bike's speedometer. Easy to read, tells you how fast you are going. Tier 1 is like knowing if you will win the race. To know that you need the speed, plus how far is left, plus how tired you are, plus what the other riders are doing. Tier 1 is the speed plus the hard extra things put together. That is why it is worth more and harder to get.
