**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Scope:** the whole codebase, every folder · **Status:** plan + flaw register (no code changed in this pass)

> **Method (what "check each line" meant here):** reading 46k lines by eye is unreliable. Instead every one of the 523 TS/TSX files in every folder was swept *mechanically* for each class of scale flaw — unbounded DB reads, tenant-scoping gaps, per-instance state, serial fan-out, ungated AI, unbounded parallelism, missing rate limits, function-duration assumptions — plus the live index coverage and the Meta call volume per sync. Every finding below carries a `file:line`. Where a first-pass flag turned out to be a false positive, it was re-checked and says so.

## 1. What "1,000 users at 100% capacity" actually means (load model)

| Dimension | Today (measured) | @1,000 DAU, full use | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Users / connected accounts | 1 / 5 | 1,000 / ~1,500 (agencies hold several) | 300× |
| Active ads in the store | ~1,018 | ~90,000 (avg ~60/account) | 90× |
| `ad_metrics` rows (90-day window) | 14,588 | **~2.5–4M** (sparse spend-days) | ~200× |
| Nightly Meta Graph calls | ~15/account → ~75 | **~15–22k** (10–15 calls/account: ads list + insights per 40-ad chunk + meta) | 300× |
| Cockpit page views | a handful | ~5–10k/day (all served from L2 cache / rollups when warm) | — |
| AI calls (chat, creative, decode) | few | **thousands/day — the dominant variable cost** | — |
| Concurrent sync work | 1 account chain | 1,500 account jobs must finish inside the nightly window | — |

**Where the ceilings actually are (in order):** (1) the **Vercel Hobby plan** (60s functions, 2 daily crons, no commercial use), (2) **PostgREST's silent 1,000-row cap** on un-paged reads, (3) **one-request fan-out loops** that can't finish in a function timeout, (4) a **global (not per-tenant) AI budget**, (5) **per-instance** rate limiting/caching on serverless, (6) **no billing/self-serve approval/deletion executor** (business blockers, not technical ones).

The data tier is **not** the ceiling: Postgres handles 4M rows trivially, and the hot indexes are already right (see §5). The scoring engine is deterministic and cached. **This app scales to 1,000 DAU with the plan below and no rewrite.**

## 3. The verdict: what breaks first at 1,000 DAU (ranked)

1. **🔴 The nightly sync silently skips every account past #1,000.** `app/api/cron/sync/route.ts:98`, `cron/rollups/route.ts:22`, `admin/rollups/route.ts:14` list `ad_accounts` with a bare `.select()` — PostgREST returns **at most 1,000 rows** and no error. At ~1,500 accounts, ~500 tenants would never sync, with no alarm. *This is the single most important finding.*
2. **🔴 Vercel Hobby.** 15 files assume `maxDuration = 300`; Hobby caps at **60s** (Pro: 300s+). Hobby also allows only daily crons and forbids commercial use. The sync survives today only because it is resumable; at scale it needs 5× more hops. **Pro is a prerequisite, not an optimization.**
3. **🔴 One-request fan-out.** `cron/rollups` (4 workers) and `admin/rollups` (serial) process **all** accounts inside one function call: 1,500 accounts × ~3s ≈ 19 min ≫ 300s. They will time out.
4. **🟠 Silent truncation on per-account reads.** Un-paged reads of `ad_meta`/`ad_metrics`/`creative_semantics` return ≤1,000 rows for big (agency) accounts → **wrong numbers, no error** (`event-roi-store.ts:16,65`, `creative/deep-analysis.ts:46,53`, `creative/decode.ts:47`, `creative-os/store.ts:36,89`, `influencer/store.ts:26`).
5. **🟠 Global AI budget = noisy neighbor.** `aiBudgetExceeded()` has no tenant; one heavy user pauses AI for all 1,000.
6. **🟠 Per-instance rate limiting.** `lib/rate-limit.ts:13` is an in-process `Map`; on serverless every instance has its own counters, so limits don't hold. The expensive routes (`ask`, `generate`, `deep-analysis`, `competitors/analyze`, `ingest/run`) have **no** rate limit at all.
7. **🟠 Business blockers.** No Stripe/metering enforcement, manual approval panel, no account-deletion executor, no email provider, no Sentry/alerts wired. These stop a *launch*, not the servers.

## 4. Flaw register — evidence, fix, better way

**A. Unbounded reads (PostgREST silently caps at 1,000 rows)**
- **A1 — CRITICAL, tenant-skipping:** `ad_accounts` listed bare in `app/api/cron/sync/route.ts:98`, `app/api/cron/rollups/route.ts:22`, `app/api/admin/rollups/route.ts:14`.
  *Fix:* page with the existing `readAllPages` (ordered by `id`). *Better way:* the cron should not list accounts at all — it should **enqueue one job per account** into the durable queue (§E) and let the drain process them; the enqueue itself is paged.
- **A2 — HIGH, wrong numbers for big accounts:** `lib/scoring/event-roi-store.ts:16,65` (`ad_meta`), `lib/creative/deep-analysis.ts:46,53` (`ad_meta`, `ad_metrics`), `lib/creative/decode.ts:47` (`creative_semantics`), `lib/creative-os/store.ts:36,89`, `lib/influencer/store.ts:26`, `lib/competitors/data.ts:85`.
  *Fix:* `readAllPages` with a total order, or an explicit `.limit()` + "showing top N" where truncation is intended. *Better way:* a lint (`check:paged-reads`) that fails any `.select()` on a table that can exceed 1,000 rows/tenant without `.range()/.limit()/.maybeSingle()` — the same class of guard as `check:rollup-boundary`.
- **A3 — fine:** `ad_sync_state`, `system_flags`, `provider_keys`, `ai_usage`, `growth_*`, `org_members/orgs/brands` are small by nature (health/admin/aggregate). `tenancy/resolve.ts` becomes a concern only for agencies with >1,000 brands.

**B. Per-instance state on serverless**
- `lib/rate-limit.ts:13` in-process `Map` — ineffective across instances. **A distributed limiter already exists** (`lib/rate-limit-distributed.ts`, with an in-proc fallback) — it needs a shared KV backend (Vercel KV / Upstash) and to be the one routes import.
- `lib/meta-sync.ts:571` L1 `LruMap(500)` — per-instance; acceptable because L2 `cockpit_cache` (DB) is shared. *Better way:* at scale, move L1 to KV so a warm view is warm everywhere, and keep the 24h SWR.
- `lib/keys.ts:18` provider-key cache — fine (short-lived, small).

**C. `maxDuration = 300` assumed in 15 files** (Hobby caps 60s): `app/app/{page,action-center,changes,creative-production,creative,media,market}`, `api/{ingest/run,admin/rollups,creative-production/shopify/sync,creative-production/generate,creative/deep-analysis,jobs/drain,cron/rollups,cron/sync}`.
  *Fix:* Vercel **Pro** (and set `maxDuration` intentionally per route). *Better way:* nothing user-facing should need 300s — long work belongs in the queue (§E); pages should stream (`<Suspense>`).

**D. Tenancy scoping** — sweep result: **0 real gaps** across all `.from(<tenant table>)` reads. The two flags were false positives (`health` = admin aggregate by design; `oauth-store.ts:11` is a keyed *write*, and `readToken(accountId, userId)` is user-scoped). The model (RLS default-deny + service-role + `.eq("user_id")`) holds. *Better way:* the full store-layer lint (Phase 5 of the master audit) so this stays true as 15 sessions add code.

**E. One-request fan-out / serial loops**
- **E1 — WILL time out:** `cron/rollups` (bounded 4 workers, but all accounts in one request) and `admin/rollups` (serial). At 1,500 accounts ≈ 19 min.
  *Fix / better way:* **use the durable queue that already exists** (`lib/queue-postgres.ts`, `claim_jobs`, `/api/jobs/drain`): the cron enqueues one `sync-account` / `rollup-account` job per account; a drain runs every few minutes (Pro allows), claims a bounded batch with a visibility timeout, retries with backoff, dead-letters failures. Each account becomes independently resumable and observable (`jobs` table). This also replaces the fragile `after()`-kicked self-chain in `cron/sync`.
- E2 — by design, keep: `ingest/ad-metrics.ts` chunked serial with a deadline; `meta-source.ts` cursor paging; `change-history.ts` incremental.

**F. AI cost control**
- Gate exists and is enforced at the source: `lib/ai/router.ts:25-26,44` (`isKilled("ai")` + `aiBudgetExceeded()`); OpenAI chat goes only through the router. ✅
- **Flaw:** `aiBudgetExceeded()` is **global** (no tenant). *Fix:* debit **per-tenant** tokens at the router (`lib/billing/meter.ts` / `token_usage` already exist for pricing) and refuse when a tenant's monthly allowance is spent; keep the global cap as the circuit breaker. *Better way:* per-tenant + per-feature quotas (chat / creative-gen / vision), surfaced in the usage meter.

**G. Unbounded parallel fan-out:** `lib/agents/creative/orchestrator.ts` (×2), `lib/growth/draft.ts`, `creative-production/providers/google-gemini.ts` — `Promise.all(items.map(aiCall))` over user-sized arrays. *Fix:* bounded concurrency (the worker-pool pattern `cron/sync` already uses), so one "generate 40 variants" request can't fire 40 simultaneous AI calls.

**H. Missing rate limits on expensive routes:** `api/ask`, `api/creative-production/generate`, `api/creative/deep-analysis`, `api/competitors/analyze`, `api/ingest/run` — product-gated but unlimited. *Fix:* per-user limits via the distributed limiter (e.g. `ask` 30/min, `generate` 10/min, `ingest/run` 2/10min). `leads` and `avatar` already have them.

## 5. Data tier — verified live, mostly ready

The RLS init-plan fix (`0040`) is applied.
- **At 1k DAU (~4M rows):** fine as-is. **Add:** a retention policy (keep 13 months hot), and plan `ad_metrics` **partitioning by month at ~20M rows** (not now). Enable **PITR** (Supabase Pro). Drop the ~15 advisor-flagged unused indexes only after real traffic confirms they stay unused.
- Supabase-js goes through PostgREST (HTTP), so Postgres connection exhaustion is **not** the risk; the 1,000-row cap (§4-A) is.

Vercel PRO (300s fns, frequent crons)          Supabase PRO (PITR, compute)

**Keep:** Next.js RSC, Supabase, the deterministic engines, rollups + self-proving, the lean 13-dep tree. **Add:** Pro tiers, KV, queue-driven ingestion, per-tenant AI quotas, distributed limits, observability, billing/approval/deletion.

## S0 — EXECUTED 2026-09-04 (remove silent truncation)

The single highest-priority correctness bug, fixed. All un-paged reads on large-per-tenant tables now page or bound.
- **A1 (the tenant-skipper):** the 3 `ad_accounts` lists (`cron/sync`, `cron/rollups`, `admin/rollups`) now use `readAllPages` (ordered), so the nightly sync can never silently skip accounts past #1,000.
- **A2:** `event-roi-store.ts` (2 `ad_meta` reads → paged; feeds the cockpit event-ROI), `influencer/store.ts` (2 reads → paged), `scope/events` (`.limit(5000)` moved inline).
- **False positives re-verified & dismissed:** `creative-os/store` (inserts), `competitors/data` (`.limit(1)`), `deep-analysis`/`decode` (`.in(ids)`-bounded), `meta-sync` (`.limit(1).maybeSingle()`).
- **Prevention:** `scripts/check-paged-reads.ts` (`check:paged-reads`, wired into `check:all`) — a statement-aware, comment-stripping lint that fails any unbounded `.select()` on the 6 large-per-tenant tables. It **caught a 4th `ad_accounts` truncation I'd missed by hand** during development, proving its worth.

**Verified:** `check:paged-reads` PASS (0 unbounded), 168/168 gate green, `check:golden` intact (scoring unchanged), tsc 0, build compiles. No product behavior changed for today's data volumes; the fix only matters once a table exceeds 1,000 rows/tenant.
