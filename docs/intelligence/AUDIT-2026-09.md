**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Method:** 8 parallel read-only subsystem auditors, each reconciling docs-vs-code (MATCH/DRIFT/UNKNOWN) with file:line evidence, under the 161-section charter. **No product code changed.**

This is deliverable #1 (full codebase audit) and the consolidated risk register (#9-16). Detail lives in the section docs: `SYSTEM-MAP.md`, `BUSINESS-LOGIC.md`, `FORMULA-LIBRARY.md`, `FAILURE-MODES.md`, `TECH-DEBT.md`, `500-LOGIC-INVENTORY.md`, and `audit-parts/*`.

## Executive verdict
AdScale's **plumbing is stronger than its intelligence.** The architecture, security foundations, and deterministic decision spine are genuinely well built. The **decisions themselves rest on unvalidated heuristics**: of 40 catalogued formulas, **~30 are evidence-level C/D (heuristic / unvalidated prior) and 0 are level A (measured from account data).** That is the single most important finding — it is exactly what charter §5/§15/§18 exist to prevent. Phase 1-2 must convert the core scores from hard-coded universal thresholds to account-relative, confidence-gated, evidence-weighted logic.

Second theme: **several safety mechanisms are built but not wired** — the financial honesty-gate, the account-median sample floor (`minAds=30`), and the creative-decode confidence floor (`0.97`) are all defined and then never called. The guardrails exist on paper, not in the live path.

## Reconciliation: the `adbrain-phase0-phase1-batch1.zip`
The zip is a **separate, unmerged** workspace's Phase 0 + Phase 1-Batch-1A. Its own manifest says not-live-verified, typecheck-blocked, check:all-partial. Its headline fix (make `lib/scoring/data-quality.ts` a wrapper around the stronger `lib/data-quality.ts`) is **NOT in the live repo** — our auditor confirmed `lib/data-quality.ts` is still dead code. **Verdict: use the zip's diagnosis as corroborating evidence (it independently found the same DQ split), treat its fixes as Phase-1 candidates to re-apply + live-verify, not as done.** Its 500-logic/library drafts are folded into our inventory as reference.

## Confirmed strengths (MATCH — do not "fix")
- Deterministic decision spine: `analyzeAccount()` + `lib/judgment` decide; AI only narrates (charter §68). Single analyzer + source adapters (the "two cockpit builders" worry was **cleared**).
- Security foundation: OAuth/Shopify tokens AES-256-GCM encrypted (env-only master key); `oauth_tokens` deny-all/service-role-only; audit_log trigger-append-only; kill-switches with env override; OAuth state/CSRF; constant-time cron secret; access-gate fails closed to WAITLIST. All 40 migration tables RLS-enabled.
- Store reads filter user+account+window; `cockpit_cache` keyed per user+account+window (§80/§81). Spend-weighted HHI diversity + fingerprint-once are correct. `/api/ask` has a real ungrounded-number veto. §79 (unknown-as-zero) **not violated** on verified paths.
